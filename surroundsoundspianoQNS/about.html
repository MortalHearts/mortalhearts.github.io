SURROUND SOUNDS PIANO_QNS ← PIANO

Surround Sounds Piano_QNS is a new instrument that plays sounds from the borough of Queens, giving people control over the sounds they are surrounded by. A Sohmer piano (manufactured in Queens) has been retrofitted and reprogrammed so each of its 88 keys plays a field recording or sample while triggering a projection. The sounds were collected from a wide variety of sources throughout Queens, from the sound of a busy restaurant in Ridgewood, to the roar of the elevated train, to rain on Houdini’s grave in Glendale.

This new instrument is one for the entire community to play. To find connections between familiar sounds and new or surprising ones. The results are sometimes beautiful, sometimes cacophonous, and sometimes a little messy — like Queens itself.

With only 88 keys and over 150 countries, 138 languages, and 2.3 million lives being lived, it’s impossible to represent all of Queens. We aimed for variety in many forms — cultural, geographical, melodic, chaotic, iconic, everyday, rehearsed, spontaneous. We hope the spirit of the project honors the sounds we’ve captured while gesturing toward those we haven’t, wherever they live, breathe, thunder, and chant in this limitless world of Queens.

Mortal Hearts is an art collective that aims to combine sound and visual to create conceptual and immersive art pieces. Its core members, Daniel Giachetti and Arthur Fleischmann, have been making various sounds together since they were kids living in their parents’ houses a few blocks away from each other.

Thank you to everyone who contributed their unique sounds to this project.
